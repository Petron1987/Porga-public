# Historical Requirements Audit

This audit records the gap between the original design and the v0.2 implementation. It preserves the design intent and provenance of decisions. Use the [current architecture](../architecture.md) for implemented capabilities and the [delivery plan](../architecture/delivery-plan.md) for remaining work.

| Source group | Design topic |
|---|---|
| C01 | Local assistants, connectivity, separate configurations and instances, and state sharing. |
| C02 | Owner goals, bounded autonomy, organization lifecycle, and independent verification. |
| C03 | Platform and domain boundaries and five-layer architecture. |
| C04 | Branding and release naming. |
| C05 | Initial implementation, evidence, and repository governance. |

The machine-readable [requirements register](requirements.json) retains all 40 identifiers, source groups, historical statuses, observed evidence, implementation gaps, and milestone references. A source group identifies an area of discussion rather than independently verified external facts. Design responses do not establish implemented features or grant authority.

The v0.2 kernel supported a fixed Research and QA workflow, deterministic gates, local state, spending limits, recovery, and proposal decisions. The wider design calls for scoped knowledge, bounded team planning, durable execution, independent experiments, controlled adoption, and rollback. The [vision](../vision.md) and [target architecture](../architecture/target.md) distinguish these capabilities and how to verify them.
