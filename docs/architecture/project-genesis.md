# A1 Project Intake and Acceptance

The owner sets the goal, success criteria, exclusions, evidence scope, budget, autonomy, escalation rules, and stop conditions. The Chief summarizes available facts and asks only questions that block a safe decision. Record assumptions and distinguish public or synthetic evidence from material that needs explicit approval.

An intake session collects missing answers, produces a versioned charter, and requests review of the exact digest. A valid answer resolves its question without silently granting authority. Until authorization, work depending on the unresolved choice remains pending. Ask the owner to resolve conflicting objectives, added costs or permissions, and uncertain effects.

Run against the approved charter and a pinned organization. The kernel checks allowed definitions, tools, capacity, costs, and an independent verifier. Amend goals, budget, or evidence through a new reviewable revision. Use early samples to clarify preferences without weakening final criteria. Owner acceptance, a verified artifact, proposal approval, and deployment each have separate records.

## 0.5 team selection acceptance

| Case | Pass criteria |
|---|---|
| O05-01 Suitability | Research and coverage audit tasks yield explainable, valid teams or graphs with independent QA. |
| O05-02 Reuse | Pin an approved definition and recheck its permissions under the new project. |
| O05-03 Missing capability | Identify affected work and stop before executing the unavailable role or tool. |
| O05-04 Invalid plan | Reject cycles, missing QA, oversized teams, and excessive cost. |
| O05-05 Revised charter | Check fresh authorization for new work and preserve the old run snapshot. |

Record the candidate, selection rationale, and deterministic verification. See [the implemented M1 slice](../m1-projects.md) and the [delivery plan](delivery-plan.md).
